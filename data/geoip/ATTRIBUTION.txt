This plugin bundles dbip-country-lite.mmdb and dbip-asn-lite.mmdb. You must retain this attribution
in distributions. The ASN database names each public peer's autonomous system and registrant
and keeps shared infrastructure out of org block rules. Reverse-DNS is off by default; kub0 does not send queries
to DB-IP or anyone else at runtime.
